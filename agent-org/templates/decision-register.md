# Decision register

A decision does not exist until it is a row here (see `OPERATING.md` section 2).

Tags: **DECIDED** (settled, never re-ask), **SUGGESTED** (recommended and in use, costly to
reverse, awaiting the owner's confirmation), **OPEN** (unresolved), **SUPERSEDED by Dn** (replaced;
keep the row).

Replace the placeholder row before adding the first decision. The placeholder is not a decision.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
